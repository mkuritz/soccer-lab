Grade: letter plus a score out of 10, ranked against the other players in the same list for this competition (10 = best matchup). Tap a grade to see its four parts, each scored 0–10 the same way: opponent matchup (how much this opponent allows of that stat), last 5 form, season hit rate, and projection vs the line. Projection: last 5 matches (40%) blended with his season (60%), pulled toward the average for his position, adjusted for the opponent and his chance of starting. For goals and assists it is his chance of at least one, capped at 85%. Line: posted prop line with its Book letter; model means no line is posted and we use 0.5 (goals, assists, goal or assist) or his season median. Hit: matches over the line. Starts: share of his last 5 and season matches he started; expected minutes is an estimate from that start rate. Season = all competitions we track for his club since the season began.
